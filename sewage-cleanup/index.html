A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Pathogens in raw sewage include salmonella, giardia, hepatitis and E. coli, along with other viruses, parasites and bacteria, and contact with anything contaminated is a serious risk to health. Across Scottsdale, Scottsdale Restoration Co answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Why Sewage Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • A drain that is slowing down should be dealt with early, while it is still partly open
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
