Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. For flood emergencies anywhere in Scottsdale, Scottsdale Restoration Co is available 24/7 to extract the water, disinfect affected areas and bring the property back safely.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Coat basement walls with waterproofing and seal any cracks in the foundation
  • Debris in a gutter or storm drain blocks proper flow, so clean them out regularly
  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • Consider flood insurance even if you are not in a designated flood zone
  • Have a backwater valve fitted to the sewer line so sewage cannot flow back inside
  • Use waterproof bins and raised shelving for anything valuable kept at ground level or below

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. This scientific approach to drying is the only reliable way to prevent secondary damage that can cost thousands more to fix.

Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • Storm damage that lets rain in through the roof or walls
  • Basement flooding after the sump pump quit in a downpour
  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • Flooring that squelches when you walk on it and walls damp to the touch
  • A water line on walls or baseboards showing how high it rose
